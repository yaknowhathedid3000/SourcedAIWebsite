Questions

Answered honestly

If yours isn't here, ask in the free Discord and Brad or a mod will answer.

Do I need trading experience to join?

No. The key levels system is taught from the ground up, and the charting sessions show you exactly how the levels get drawn before the week starts. That said, this is a live room, not a beginner course. If you have never placed a trade, start in the free Discord, sit in on a couple of sessions, and join the paid room when it clicks. Most people are trading alongside Brad within their first week.

What's the difference between the free Discord and the paid community?

The free Discord is the lobby: general chat, market talk, and occasional posts from Brad. The paid community is the room itself: live trading five days a week, the Sunday and Wednesday charting sessions, the full key levels system, and Brad's marked-up levels before each session. If you want to watch the trades happen, you need to be in the paid room.

How is this different from Levels Mastery?

The Payout Room is the group. Levels Mastery is Brad's 1-on-1 mentorship, where he works through your own trades, your risk, and your account plan directly with you. It is application only and priced accordingly. Almost everyone starts in the community first, and Brad invites traders to apply from inside the room.

How often are the live sessions?

Live trading runs Monday through Friday during the US market session. Charting sessions run Sunday, to mark the levels for the week, and Wednesday, to re-mark them mid-week. That is seven live touchpoints a week. Times are posted in the Discord, and sessions are recorded so you can catch up if you miss one.
