Privacy Policy

The Payout Room ("we", "us") is operated by Brad Scholten, trading as Savvy B Trades. This policy explains what we collect when you visit this site or join the community, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us when you join: name, email address, and the Discord username you connect.
  • Payment details are collected and stored by our payment processor (Stripe, Whop, or Skool, depending on the checkout you use). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Text messaging. If you give us your mobile number and tick the consent box, we will text you your access link, session reminders, and community updates. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for their own marketing.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and device type, collected through standard analytics so we can see what is working on the site.

How we use it

  • To give you access to the community, sessions, and Discord.
  • To bill your membership and send receipts, trial reminders, and account notices.
  • To send occasional emails about sessions, charting breakdowns, and community updates. You can unsubscribe from these at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the community: our payment processor, our email provider, Discord, and our analytics provider. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Cancel your membership from your account page or by emailing us, and your access ends at the close of the billing period.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support email listed inside the community Discord, or by direct message to @savvybtrades on Instagram.
